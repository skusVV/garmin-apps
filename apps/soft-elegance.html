How to set Soft Elegance on your Garmin watch

  1. Open the Garmin Connect IQ Store listing for Soft Elegance
  2. Tap Try to start the 24-hour free trial
  3. Sync your Garmin watch via the Garmin Connect app
  4. In the Garmin Connect app, go to Watch Faces → My Watch Faces, select Soft Elegance, and send it to your watch - or on the watch, long-press the current watch face and choose it from Connect IQ Store
  5. Pick your color theme from the watch face's own settings

Frequently asked questions

What makes Soft Elegance different from a default watch face?

A refined, clutter-free layout with fine typography and soft color palettes, built to look premium in the boardroom and still show the fitness data you check throughout the day.

How do I set it as my watch face?

In the Garmin Connect app, go to Watch Faces → My Watch Faces, select Soft Elegance, and send it to your watch. On the watch itself, you can also long-press the current watch face and choose it from the Connect IQ Store.

What data does it show?

High-contrast displays for calories, distance, and battery percentage, plus heart rate and intensity minutes, with subtle integrated circular gauges tracking your daily goals.

Can I change the color theme?

Yes. Choose from 7 curated palettes, including Sage Green, Rose Gold, Lavender, Mint, and Sandy Tan, to match your strap or outfit.

Does it work well with always-on display?

Yes. It's designed to look sharp with the display always on while staying efficient on battery life.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Soft Elegance includes a 24-hour free trial. After the trial, unlock forever for $1.99 - one-time payment, no subscription.
