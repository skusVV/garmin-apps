BJJ (Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu)

Watch App Jiu-Jitsu Combat Sports

Track every Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu session from your wrist. Built for grapplers who want real data about their training load - not guesswork.

What it tracks

  • Session duration - know exactly how long you've been on the mat
  • Real-time heart rate - monitor intensity during drilling and sparring
  • Calories burned - fuel your recovery properly
  • Training Effect score - understand how hard you actually worked
  • Heart rate chart - review your intensity curve after each session
  • Recovery time - know when you're ready to roll again

After your session

  • Swipe through your post-session summary: duration, training effect, calories, and recovery time
  • Syncs automatically to Garmin Connect for long-term training history

How to use

  • Press START to begin recording
  • Press START again to pause and access the stop menu
  • Screen lock - while rolling, press START and select Lock Screen from the menu; the session keeps recording with buttons disabled. Hold START for 3 seconds to unlock
  • Save when done - your session is stored as a training activity in Garmin Connect

Latest updates

  • September 1, 2026 - activity type is now recorded as Boxing instead of Strength Training, and can be changed to Strength or Cardio Training in the Garmin Connect app settings.
  • June 3, 2026 - added screen lock to prevent accidental button presses during training, plus fixes for occasional HR chart sample drops, a lock-screen timer restart bug, and the heart rate sensor continuing to run after saving or discarding a session.

Frequently asked questions

What does BJJ track?

Session duration, real-time heart rate, calories burned, a Training Effect score, a heart rate chart, and recovery time - everything syncs automatically to Garmin Connect.

What activity type does a session save as?

Boxing, as of the September 2026 update (it was previously recorded as Strength Training). You can change it to Strength or Cardio Training afterwards in the Garmin Connect app settings if you prefer.

How do I use it?

Press START to begin recording. Press START again to pause and access the stop menu. Save when done and your session is stored as a training activity in Garmin Connect.

Can I lock the screen while rolling?

Yes. Press START and select Lock Screen from the menu - the session keeps recording with every button disabled. Hold START for 3 seconds to unlock.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. BJJ includes a 7-day free trial. After the trial, unlock forever for $1.99 - one-time payment, no subscription, no ads.
