Frequently asked questions

Why does it save as Muay Thai instead of a generic cardio activity?

Garmin Connect supports a dedicated Muay Thai activity type. This app uses it, so your sessions appear correctly labelled in your activity history, training load, and weekly summaries - not lumped in with generic cardio or "other" workouts.

What do I see during a session?

A large, readable duration timer and your real-time heart rate, so you can gauge intensity through every round without breaking focus.

What is recorded after the session?

Duration, average heart rate, calories, and training effect, plus a full-session heart rate chart. Everything is saved to Garmin Connect as a Muay Thai activity.

Can I pause mid-session?

Yes. You can pause, resume, or discard a session at any time, using either buttons or the touchscreen depending on your device.

Does it work on a touchscreen watch?

Yes. The interface works with both buttons and touchscreen, and uses a pure black background that suits AMOLED displays.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Muay Thai includes a 72-hour free trial. After the trial, unlock forever for $1.99 - one-time payment, no subscription.
