MealGap - Meal Timing & Metabolic Health

Watch App Health Nutrition

You fast overnight, then graze all day. MealGap closes that gap. Intermittent fasting works, but many people nail the overnight fast and then snack from breakfast to bedtime - a coffee here, a handful of nuts there, a bite while cooking. Each of those is a separate signal to your body, and constant grazing quietly undoes much of the metabolic benefit you were chasing.

The science of the gap

  • Insulin rest - every time you eat, insulin rises to store the incoming fuel. Space meals a few hours apart and it can fall between them, giving your metabolism room to switch from storing to burning.
  • Gut housekeeping - when the gut is empty for a couple of hours, a natural cleanup wave (the migrating motor complex) sweeps it clean. Non-stop snacking never lets it run; real breaks do.
  • Steadier energy - fewer eating occasions mean fewer glucose spikes and crashes across the day. It's not about eating less or skipping meals - it's about giving your body real breaks between meals.

What MealGap does

  • One-tap log - press once each time you eat; logged the wrong time? Fix it in history
  • Calm status - see how long since your last meal in gentle colors: Digesting, Settling, Clean gap. There is deliberately no red - eating is never a failure
  • Today rhythm - a simple dot strip shows every eating occasion across your day at a glance
  • Stats (7 days) - meals per day, your average daytime gap, and your average overnight gap (your natural fasting window)
  • Editable history - review and correct the last 7 days; every edit recalculates your stats

Watch app plus glance - log from either one with a single tap. Works on both touch and button-only watches.

Frequently asked questions

What does MealGap actually track?

The time between your meals, not just your overnight fast. Every tap logs an eating occasion, and MealGap shows how long it's been since the last one, your meals-per-day count, and your average daytime and overnight gaps over the last 7 days.

Is this the same as intermittent fasting tracking?

It's a companion to it. Intermittent fasting focuses on your overnight window; MealGap focuses on the gaps between meals during the day, which is where grazing quietly undoes the benefit of a clean overnight fast. Pair it with Fasting Tracker if you do both.

Does MealGap work without a phone?

Yes. Logging works from the watch app or its glance with one tap, on both touch and button-only watches.
