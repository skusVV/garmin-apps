Frequently asked questions

What does Barre Workout Tracker record during a session?

A live duration timer, your real-time heart rate, and your calorie burn adding up in real time - all readable at a glance throughout class.

What are guided classes?

Five presets - Standard, Express, Long, Lower Body, and Core Focus - that automatically walk you through each segment (Warm-up, Thighs, Seat, Core, Stretch) with automatic timing and a chime between segments, so you don't have to watch the clock.

What is recorded after class?

Duration, calories burned, Training Effect score, recovery time estimate, average/max/min heart rate, a dominant HR zone with a time-in-zone breakdown, a full heart rate chart, and - for guided classes - a per-segment breakdown.

Can I see my past workouts?

Yes. Every session is saved to workout history - reopen any past class from your watch to see its full summary again.

Is there a weekly goal?

Yes. The This Week view tracks your class count against a weekly goal so you can see your progress at a glance.

Can I pause mid-session?

Yes. You can pause, resume, or discard a session at any time, using either buttons or the touchscreen depending on your device.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Barre Workout Tracker includes a 7-day free trial. After the trial, unlock forever for $2.99 - one-time payment, no subscription.
