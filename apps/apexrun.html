ApexRun – Pace, HR Zones & Cadence

Data Field Running

ApexRun is a full-screen running data field that shows every metric you glance at during a run on one clean, AMOLED-optimized screen - pace, distance, heart rate zone, cadence, and more, with no scrolling and no clutter.

A color-coded heart rate zone arc curves across the top so you read your effort without reading a number. Your active zone glows brighter, and a live marker shows exactly where your heart rate sits across the range.

What's on screen

  • Heart rate zone arc with a live current-HR position marker
  • Big distance readout in km or miles, auto-matched to your watch units
  • Current running pace per km/mi, smoothed for a steady, readable number
  • Average pace, to instantly see if you're speeding up or slowing down
  • Duration with a blinking recording indicator
  • Cadence, average heart rate, and calories
  • Time of day, always on screen

Built for glanceability

  • True-black AMOLED background for battery efficiency and zero glare
  • A clear color system - white for live values, your accent color for reference values, grey for labels - so your eye locks onto the right number in a split second
  • 10 accent colors to personalize from Garmin Connect: aqua, lime, amber, blue, red, pink, purple, yellow, green, or white

How to add ApexRun to your Garmin watch

  1. Open the Garmin Connect IQ Store listing for ApexRun and tap Get it
  2. Sync your Garmin watch via the Garmin Connect app
  3. In the Garmin Connect app, open your running activity's Data Screens editor and add ApexRun as a full-screen data field to one of your screens
  4. On the watch, you can also hold the up/down button (or open the menu) during a run, choose Edit, and add ApexRun to a data screen directly
  5. Personalize your accent color from the same settings screen in Garmin Connect

Frequently asked questions

What is a Connect IQ data field?

A data field is a small metric overlay you add to one of your existing activity data screens - it isn't an app you open or start on its own, and it doesn't save an activity by itself. ApexRun replaces or joins the numbers on your running data screen; your watch's own activity app still records the run.

What does ApexRun show?

A heart rate zone arc with a live position marker, distance, current and average pace, duration with a recording indicator, cadence, average heart rate, calories, and the time of day - all on one full-screen, AMOLED-optimized layout.

How do I add it to my watch?

Add it as a data field to one of your running activity's data screens through the Garmin Connect app's Data Screens editor, or directly on the watch by holding the up/down button (or opening the menu) during a run and choosing Edit.

Can I change the colors?

Yes. Pick from 10 accent colors - aqua, lime, amber, blue, red, pink, purple, yellow, green, or white - from Garmin Connect's app settings.

Is it free?

Yes. ApexRun is currently free to add to your watch.
