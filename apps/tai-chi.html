Tai Chi Tracker

Watch App Mindfulness Martial Arts

Tai Chi deserves its own activity - not a Yoga entry in your feed. Tai Chi Tracker records every practice as a dedicated Tai Chi activity in Garmin Connect, and it tracks what practitioners actually care about: rounds, pacing, breathing, and a calm heart rate.

Built for Taiji, Taijiquan, Qigong, and standing practice. Native data-field layout, pure black AMOLED background, no clutter - and it works with both buttons and touchscreen.

Two ways to train

  • Open Training - no time limit, practice as long as you want
  • Training with Goal - set a target duration, feel a gentle vibration when you reach it, and keep going into overtime if you wish

Count your rounds, not just your minutes

  • Press LAP to close a round; each one is saved as a lap in Garmin Connect
  • The transition screen shows the round you just finished and how it compares to your average pace, so you know instantly whether you rushed it
  • A round-by-round breakdown after every session, with split times and your average round length

During practice

  • Live heart rate zone arc, readable at a glance
  • Bell timer - optional subtle vibration every 5, 10, 15, 20, or 30 minutes for form transitions or breathing checkpoints
  • Screen lock - no accidental taps in the middle of a form
  • Pause, resume, or discard at any time

After practice

  • Duration, calories, training effect, and recovery time
  • Minimum heart rate and breathing rate - the numbers that show how calm the session really was
  • HR zone breakdown with histogram, time and percentage per zone
  • Round splits alongside your average round
  • Full-session heart rate chart

Keep the habit

  • Weekly view - see which days you practiced this week
  • Practice history - your last 20 sessions with date, duration and rounds; open any one to see its stats again

Frequently asked questions

Why does it save as Tai Chi instead of Yoga or Other?

Garmin Connect supports a dedicated Tai Chi activity type. Tai Chi Tracker uses it so your practices appear correctly labelled in your activity history, training load, and weekly summaries - not lumped in with yoga or generic workouts.

How does round tracking work?

Press LAP to close a round. Each round is saved as a lap in Garmin Connect, and a transition screen shows the round you just finished against your average pace. After the session you get a round-by-round breakdown with split times and your average round length.

What metrics are tracked?

During practice: a live heart rate zone arc and your running average heart rate. After practice: duration, calories, training effect, recovery time, minimum heart rate, breathing rate, Stress Delta, an HR zone breakdown with histogram and time and percentage per zone, round splits, and a full-session heart rate chart.

Can I set a target duration?

Yes. Training with Goal lets you set a target; you feel a gentle vibration when you reach it and can keep going into overtime. Open Training has no time limit at all.

What is the bell timer for?

It is an optional subtle vibration every 5, 10, 15, 20, or 30 minutes - useful as a cue for form transitions or breathing checkpoints without having to look at the watch.

Can I stop the screen reacting to accidental taps?

Yes. Open the menu during a session and choose Lock Screen. An amber LOCKED badge appears at the bottom of the screen; hold START for 3 seconds to unlock.

Can I pause mid-session?

Yes. You can pause, resume, or discard a practice at any time from the watch - using either buttons or the touchscreen, depending on your device.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Tai Chi Tracker includes a 5-day free trial. After the trial, unlock forever for $2.99 - one-time payment, no subscription.
