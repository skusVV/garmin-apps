Kickboxing Round Timer & Tracker

Watch App Kickboxing Combat Sports

Train real rounds, not a stopwatch. Kickboxing turns your Garmin into a proper round timer and records every session as a real Garmin activity - so your work shows up in Garmin Connect with heart rate, calories, Training Effect and recovery time.

Guided round timer (work/rest)

  • Beginner - 3 rounds, 2 min work / 1 min rest
  • Standard - 5 rounds, 3 min work / 1 min rest
  • HIIT - 8 rounds, 2 min work / 1 min rest
  • Endurance - 10 rounds, 3 min work / 1 min rest
  • Custom - set your own work, rest, and round count
  • Rounds advance automatically; a full-screen ROUND / REST card shows what's next, with an optional 10-second warning before every change

Open session for bag work and sparring

  • No structure needed - start an open session and let it run
  • Optional interval bell every 1, 2, or 3 minutes to keep you honest on the heavy bag

Live on screen

  • Big colour-coded countdown - orange for work, blue for rest
  • Round X of Y always visible
  • Live heart rate

After the session

  • Duration, calories, Training Effect, and recovery time
  • Average, max, and min heart rate
  • Per-round breakdown - time plus average and max HR for every round
  • Time in heart rate zones Z1-Z5
  • Full heart rate graph for the whole session

History and weekly goal

  • Your last 20 sessions stay on the watch - open any one to see its full summary again
  • Set a weekly goal of 2 to 5 sessions; the This Week screen shows your training days at a glance

Frequently asked questions

What round schemes are built in?

Beginner (3 rounds, 2 min work / 1 min rest), Standard (5 rounds, 3/1), HIIT (8 rounds, 2/1), Endurance (10 rounds, 3/1), or Custom - set your own work, rest, and round count. Rounds advance automatically with a full-screen ROUND / REST card and an optional 10-second warning before each change.

Can I use it without a fixed round structure?

Yes. Start an open session for bag work or sparring and let it run freely, with an optional interval bell every 1, 2, or 3 minutes.

Does it save as a kickboxing activity in Garmin Connect?

Yes. Sessions record correctly as a boxing/kickboxing activity, with average, max, and min heart rate on the summary alongside duration, calories, Training Effect, and recovery time.

What do I see during a round?

A big colour-coded countdown - orange for work, blue for rest - the current round out of the total, and your live heart rate.

What is in the per-round breakdown?

Time plus average and max heart rate for every single round, alongside time spent in heart rate zones Z1-Z5 and a full heart rate graph for the whole session.

Is it only for kickboxing?

No. It's also well suited to boxing, Muay Thai, MMA striking, and general heavy-bag workouts.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Kickboxing Round Timer & Tracker includes a 7-day free trial. After the trial, unlock forever for $2.49 - one-time payment, no subscription.
