Blood Pressure Journal

Watch App Health Blood Pressure

Log readings from your blood pressure monitor on your Garmin watch - no phone needed. Use the UP/DOWN buttons to enter systolic, diastolic, and pulse in seconds. Every reading is instantly colour-coded by AHA zone.

Requires a blood pressure cuff or monitor - the watch does not measure blood pressure itself.

Features

  • Fast scroll entry - UP/DOWN buttons for systolic, diastolic, and pulse
  • Instant zone feedback - Normal, Elevated, High, Very High, Danger
  • History at a glance - 5 most recent readings; press START to scroll all records
  • Calendar heatmap - monthly view coloured by your worst zone each day
  • Smart reminders - configurable morning and evening notifications
  • Email export - send your full log to any address with one tap
  • Glance view - last reading and zone visible in the app list without opening the app
  • Stores up to 200 readings on-device, no cloud required

AHA blood pressure zones

  • Normal - below 120/80
  • Elevated - systolic 120-129
  • High - 130-139 / 80-89
  • Very High - 140+ / 90+
  • Danger - above 180/120

Privacy

  • All readings are stored on your watch only
  • No cloud account, no server, no analytics
  • Email export only fires when you explicitly tap it

Blood Pressure Journal is for personal tracking and informational purposes only. It is not a medical device and does not measure, diagnose, treat, or replace medical advice. Requires a separate blood pressure cuff or monitor. Consult a qualified healthcare professional regarding blood pressure management.

How to install Blood Pressure Journal on your Garmin watch

  1. Open the Garmin Connect IQ Store listing for Blood Pressure Journal
  2. Tap Try to start the 7-day free trial
  3. Sync your Garmin watch via the Garmin Connect app
  4. To enable email export: Garmin Connect → My Apps → Blood Pressure Journal → Settings → enter your email address
  5. Open the app on your watch and use UP/DOWN to log your first reading

Frequently asked questions

Does the watch measure my blood pressure?

No. Blood Pressure Journal is a logger, not a sensor - it requires a separate blood pressure cuff or monitor. You enter the systolic, diastolic, and pulse readings from that device using the watch's UP/DOWN buttons.

What are the AHA zones?

Based on American Heart Association ranges: Normal (below 120/80), Elevated (systolic 120-129), High (130-139 / 80-89), Very High (140+ / 90+), and Danger (above 180/120). Every reading is instantly colour-coded against these zones.

Can I share my readings with my doctor?

Yes. One tap in the app emails your full log - every reading with its zone - to the address you configure in Garmin Connect settings.

Is my blood pressure data private?

Yes. All readings are stored on your watch only. There is no cloud account, no automatic sync, and no analytics collection. The email export only sends data when you explicitly tap it.

How many readings can it store?

Up to 200 readings on-device. The History view shows your 5 most recent at a glance, and you can press START to scroll through every stored record.

Can it remind me to take a reading?

Yes. Smart reminders can be configured for a morning and an evening notification.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Blood Pressure Journal includes a 7-day free trial. After the trial, unlock forever for $3.99 - one-time payment, no subscription.
