Qigong

Watch App Mindfulness Wellness

Qigong turns your watch into a simple, focused session tracker for your practice - start, move, breathe, and let it record the rest. Press start and the app records your session as a yoga-type activity in Garmin Connect.

What it captures

  • Duration - every minute of your practice
  • Heart rate - live BPM throughout the session
  • Calories - energy burned as you move

When you finish

  • A clean summary of total duration, training effect, calories, and recovery time
  • Swipe to a heart-rate graph that traces how your body responded from the first movement to the last breath

Distraction-free by design

  • No menus to fight, no clutter
  • A calm black screen with large, glanceable numbers keeps you present in your practice instead of staring at your wrist
  • Pause and resume anytime; save or discard when you're done

How it works

  • Press START to begin
  • Press SELECT to pause, then choose Resume, Save, or Discard
  • Saved sessions sync to Garmin Connect as a yoga activity, so they count toward your training history and recovery

Compatible Garmin watches

How to install Qigong on your Garmin watch

  1. Open the Garmin Connect IQ Store listing for Qigong
  2. Tap Try to start the 7-day free trial
  3. Sync your Garmin watch via the Garmin Connect app
  4. Open Qigong on your watch and press START to begin a session

Frequently asked questions

What does Qigong track?

Duration, live heart rate, and calories during your practice. Afterwards you get a summary with training effect, calories, and recovery time, plus a full heart-rate graph of the session.

Does it save as a Qigong activity in Garmin Connect?

Sessions save as a yoga-type activity in Garmin Connect, so they count toward your training history and recovery.

How do I use it?

Press START to begin. Press SELECT to pause, then choose Resume, Save, or Discard. There are no other menus to navigate mid-session.

Why is the screen so plain?

By design. A calm black screen with large, glanceable numbers keeps you present in your practice instead of staring at your wrist working out a cluttered display.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Qigong includes a full 7-day free trial with every feature unlocked, no commitment. After the trial, unlock forever for a one-time $1.99 payment. No subscription.
