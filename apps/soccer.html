Soccer Tracker

Watch App Soccer Team Sports

Soccer Tracker turns your Garmin watch into a complete match-day companion. Record every soccer match and training session as a native activity, keep the score on your wrist, and see your GPS map, heart-rate zones, and running stats in Garmin Connect afterwards - built for players who want real football data, not a generic stopwatch.

Full match mode

  • Two halves, a half-time break, and optional extra time
  • Authentic count-up clock with stoppage time, just like the real game
  • Free training mode for drills and practice

Live on screen

  • Live score for both teams, right on the watch
  • Heart rate with a live 5-zone color arc
  • Distance, top speed, and estimated sprints from built-in GPS
  • Calories, average and max heart rate

Simple controls

  • UP / DOWN - switch between live screens: match clock + score, performance stats, and heart-rate zones
  • START - open the match menu: add a goal for either team, edit the score, end the half, lock the screen, or save & finish
  • Lock the screen by holding BACK for 3 seconds - the display stays fully visible but every button is ignored, so no accidental taps under a sleeve or in a pocket. Hold any button for 3 seconds to unlock

After the match

  • Saves as a real Soccer activity in Garmin Connect
  • GPS map of your movement and per-half laps
  • Custom fields: final score, time in each heart-rate zone, dominant zone, distance, max speed, and sprint count

Make it yours

  • Set half length, break length, extra-time on/off and length, sprint sensitivity, and vibration alerts in Settings

Compatible Garmin watches

Frequently asked questions

What match structure does it support?

Full match mode with two halves, a half-time break, and optional extra time, plus an authentic count-up clock with stoppage time. There's also a free training mode with no structure, for drills and practice.

How do I keep score during a match?

Press START to open the match menu: add a goal for either team, edit the score, end the half, lock the screen, or save & finish. The live score for both teams stays on screen throughout.

Can I lock the screen during a match?

Yes. Hold BACK for 3 seconds to lock - the display stays fully visible but every button is ignored, so there are no accidental taps under a sleeve or in a pocket. Hold any button for 3 seconds to unlock.

Does it save as a real soccer activity?

Yes. Sessions save as a Soccer activity in Garmin Connect, with a GPS map of your movement, per-half laps, and custom fields for final score, time in each heart-rate zone, dominant zone, distance, max speed, and sprint count.

Do I need a GPS watch?

Distance, top speed, and sprint count require a watch with built-in GPS - sprint count is estimated from GPS speed. Score, heart rate, and calories work on any compatible watch.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Soccer Tracker includes a free 24-hour trial with every feature unlocked. After the trial, unlock forever for $2.99 - one-time payment, no subscription.
